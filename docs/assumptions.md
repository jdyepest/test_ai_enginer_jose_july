# Assumptions

- Human approval remains mandatory before any generated material is distributed.
- Meetings are uploaded or saved into a controlled intake location.
- Google Drive event integration is production wiring; the local POC simulates it with HTTP.
- Transcript quality directly affects summary quality.
- Audio and video transcription cost scales with minutes processed.
- Supporting operational data from Shopify, Lightspeed, Gorgias, and Slack is future scope.
- The POC stores local artifacts for review and audit, not long-term records management.
