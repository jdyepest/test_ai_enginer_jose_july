# Client Overview

This workflow turns leadership and operations meetings into review-ready materials.

A meeting enters the system when a transcript or recording is placed in the intake location. The system reads the transcript or creates one from audio/video, identifies the main themes, extracts three objectives, captures three action items, and creates an editable PowerPoint deck.

The user receives a folder containing a structured JSON summary, normalized transcript, and editable deck. The materials are not automatically sent to Slack, email, or customers. A person reviews the output first and decides what can be edited, approved, or rejected.

Human judgment is still required for accuracy, tone, business context, approval, and distribution. The system helps prepare materials faster; it does not replace leadership review.

Phase two could add production Google Drive events, Slack reviewer notifications, richer operational data, and dashboard reporting after the meeting-intelligence pilot proves quality and usefulness.
